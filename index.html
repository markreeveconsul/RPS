HOW TO PLAY

Both sides start with 100 HP. Each winning throw deals 20 damage. Ties deal no damage. Take the computer to zero to win.

  • Rock crushes scissors.
  • Paper covers rock.
  • Scissors cut paper.

Click a hand or press 1, 2, 3. The computer picks randomly before you choose. All feedback is visible, so sound is optional. Match wins carry across rematches until you refresh or close the page. Restarting an unfinished match does not change the score.
